Our philosophy

We cook what the week gives us.

Every morning our farmers, fishermen and foragers send word of what is at its peak. The menu is written after that, never before. It is a quiet way of cooking: few ingredients, patient technique, and an honest sense of place.

Behind the kitchen

Chef Meera Raghavan

Trained in Chennai and Kyoto, Meera opened Stackly after fifteen years in other people’s kitchens. She cooks with restraint: one idea per plate, finished by hand.
